SAMPLE PROPOSAL B

Company: Bright Horizon Solar Services

The company proposes to install a commercial rooftop solar
system for a warehouse in Windsor, Ontario.

The company reports six commercial solar installations
larger than 100 kW completed during the last four years.

Total proposed price: CAD 189,500.
Estimated completion: 10 weeks.

WSIB clearance is confirmed.
Liability insurance is CAD 5,000,000.

Panel warranty: 25 years.
Workmanship warranty: 5 years.

A project coordinator will be assigned at kickoff.
The proposal states that electrical work will be performed
by a licensed subcontractor. The bidder's own Ontario
electrical contractor licence is not provided.

The crew details, safety plan, and monitoring approach
require further clarification.
